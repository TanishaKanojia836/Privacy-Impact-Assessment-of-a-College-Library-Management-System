## 1. Aim

To conduct a Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) of a College Library Management System, identify potential privacy risks, assess those risks, and recommend suitable mitigation measures.

---

## 2. Introduction

A Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) is a process used to identify and evaluate privacy risks associated with a technology or system.

This project performs a PIA on a College Library Management System that allows students to search, borrow, return, and reserve books electronically.

---

## 3. Objectives

The objectives of this assessment are:

- Identify personal information collected by the system.
- Understand how personal information flows through the system.
- Identify potential privacy risks.
- Assess the likelihood and impact of those risks.
- Develop mitigation strategies.
- Recommend privacy and security controls.

---

## 4. System Selected

The system selected for this assessment is a College Library Management System.

The system allows students to:

- Log in using their student ID.
- Search for books.
- Borrow and return books.
- Reserve books.
- View borrowing history.
- Receive library notifications.

Librarians use the system to manage books, student accounts, borrowing records, and fines.

---

## 5. Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Role |
|---|---|
| Students | Use library services |
| Librarians | Manage library records |
| IT Department | Maintain the system |
| College Administration | Manage policies and records |
| Software Vendor | Provides/maintains software |

---

## 6. Personal Information

The system may process:

- Student name
- Student ID
- Email address
- Phone number
- Login information
- Books borrowed
- Borrowing dates
- Return dates
- Reservation history
- Fine information
- Security logs

---

## 7. Privacy Risks

The main privacy risks identified are:

1. Unauthorized access to student accounts.
2. Database breaches.
3. Excessive collection of personal information.
4. Excessive retention of student information.
5. Unauthorized employee access.
6. Weak authentication.
7. Third-party/vendor access.
8. Accidental disclosure of student information.

---

## 8. Risk Assessment

Risks were evaluated using:

**Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact**

The detailed risk assessment is available in [Risk Assessment](05-risk-assessment.md).

---

## 9. Mitigation Strategies

The following measures are recommended:

- Strong authentication
- Multi-factor authentication
- Encryption
- Role-based access control
- Data minimization
- Data retention policies
- Vendor privacy requirements
- Security monitoring
- Privacy notices
- Incident-response procedures

## 11. Action Plan

The college should:

- Implement strong authentication.
- Restrict employee access.
- Encrypt sensitive information.
- Establish retention periods.
- Review third-party access.
- Provide students with a privacy notice.
- Establish a privacy incident-response procedure.

---

## 12. Conclusion

The PIA identified several privacy risks associated with the College Library Management System.

The major risks involve unauthorized access, data breaches, excessive data collection, excessive retention, and third-party access.

The recommended controls can reduce these risks and help protect student privacy.

The PIA should be reviewed whenever significant changes are made to the system.
