# 05 - Risk Assessment

## Risk Calculation

Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact

### Scale

| Score | Risk Level |
|---|---|
| 1-4 | Low |
| 5-9 | Medium |
| 10-15 | High |
| 16-25 | Very High |
