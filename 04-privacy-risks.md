# 04 - Privacy Risks

## Risk 1: Unauthorized Account Access

An attacker could obtain a student's password and access their library account.

### Potential Impact

The attacker could view or modify the student's information.

---

## Risk 2: Database Breach

An attacker could gain unauthorized access to the library database.

### Potential Impact

Student personal information and library records could be exposed.

---

## Risk 3: Excessive Data Collection

The system may collect information that is not necessary for library services.

### Example

Continuous location tracking would normally not be necessary for borrowing books.

---

## Risk 4: Excessive Data Retention

The college may keep old student information longer than necessary.

### Potential Impact

The amount of personal information exposed in a future breach could increase.

---

## Risk 5: Unauthorized Employee Access

Employees may access information that they do not need for their job.

---

## Risk 6: Vendor Access

An external software or cloud provider may have access to student information.

---

## Risk 7: Accidental Disclosure

A student's borrowing history could accidentally be sent to another student.

---

## Risk 8: Weak Authentication

Weak passwords could make student accounts easier to compromise.
